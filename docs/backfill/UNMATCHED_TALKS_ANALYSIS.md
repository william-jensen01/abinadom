## Categories

### 1. Sustaining of Officers (~109 entries)
Every conference (April and October) from 1971–2025 has an unmatched sustaining item. Title and URI format vary over time:

- "Sustaining of Church Authorities and Officers" (early 1970s)
- "The Sustaining of Church Officers" (1974–2018)
- "Sustaining of General Authorities, Area Seventies, and General Officers" (2019–present)
- Recent conferences use numeric URIs (e.g., `/21oaks`, `/21eyring`)

### 2. Statistical Reports (~47 entries)
April conferences only, 1971–2017. The Church **discontinued publishing a statistical report at conference after 2017**.

### 3. Audit / Finance Reports (~54 entries)
April conferences only, every year 1971–2024. Three name variants over history:

| Era | Name |
|-----|------|
| 1971–1972 | "Audit Report" |
| 1974–1980 | "Church Finance Committee Report" (separate finance committee era) |
| 1981–2000 | "Church Audit Committee Report" |
| 2001–present | "Church Auditing Department Report" |

### 4. Welfare Services Addresses (5 entries, 1974–1975)
Addresses given at a separate Saturday Welfare Services session by Marion G. Romney, N. Eldon Tanner, and Spencer W. Kimball. This session was discontinued after 1975.

### 5. Solemn Assemblies (3–4 entries)

- 1974/04 — The Solemn Assembly
- 1986/04 — Solemn Assembly and Sustaining of Church Officers
- 1994/10 — The Solemn Assembly Sustaining of Church Officers
- 1995/04 — The Solemn Assembly Sustaining of Church Officers

## Notable Exceptions

Two categories have substantive doctrinal content and may warrant special handling:

- **The Family: A Proclamation to the World** (1995/10) — a canonical doctrinal document presented at conference, not a talk but arguably more significant than a statistical report.
- **Welfare Services Addresses** (1974–1975) — substantive addresses by First Presidency members at a session that no longer exists; excluded because the session type isn't in the DB.
